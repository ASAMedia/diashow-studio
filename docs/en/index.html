Slideshows like on the Mac – for Windows

Pick photos and videos, add a theme and music, done: Diashow Studio turns them into a slideshow and saves it as an MP4 video. For the school trip, the graduation or the open day.

  • Free
  • No account
  • Works offline
  • Open source
Download for free Read the handbook

For Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) · German and English

What Diashow Studio does

Modelled on the slideshow in Photos on the Mac: a few clicks to a finished show, and everything adjustable if you like. An assistant leads to the first slideshow in three steps.

Themes and looks

11 themes that show off your photos

The seven themes of Photos on the Mac – Ken Burns, Origami, Reflections, Sliding Panels, Vintage Prints, Classic and Magazine – and four of our own: Retro Film, Scrapbook, Snapshots and Shatter.

  • Several photos per slide are arranged automatically; layout, framing and a free collage are yours to change.
  • Eight looks such as Warm, Mono or Noir colour only the photos and videos.
  • Titles, chapters from the capture date or folder names, text boxes and end credits.
Music

Music that fits the show

Your own music (MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC and more) as a playlist with crossfades.

  • If you like, the slideshow lasts exactly as long as the music.
  • Slide changes on the beat.
  • The music gets quieter by itself while a video plays its sound; songs and video sound are evened out.
  • Songs per chapter – say, for each day of the school trip.
Review

Sort out hundreds of photos quickly

After a school trip there are easily 600 photos and videos. Review shows them all in a grid, and you go through them with the keyboard.

  • X leaves a picture out of the show, 1–5 give stars, Space shows it large.
  • The app finds blurry photos and duplicates – even the same video twice, e.g. from WhatsApp.
Privacy

Pixelate faces

For pupils without photo consent: click a detected face or drag out an area.

  • Pixelate this person everywhere finds the person in every photo and video of the show.
  • In videos the face is followed through the whole clip.
  • Before exporting, Check faces lists the slides with faces that aren't pixelated yet.
  • Detection runs offline on your PC.

Always check the result yourself: detection doesn't find every face, e.g. in profile or very small ones.

Share over Wi-Fi

Collect photos by QR code

The laptop shows a QR code, and the class uploads photos and videos from their phones straight into the slideshow – on the local Wi-Fi, without internet and without an app.

  • The other way round, the finished video can go to the phones, also as a small 720p version.
  • The server only runs while the dialog is open and only answers links with a random key.
Export

Save it as a video – up to 4K

MP4 (H.264 or HEVC) with presets for YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp/e‑mail and TV/projector.

  • Exports run in the background while you keep working, and continue after a crash.
  • A graphics card by NVIDIA, Intel or AMD makes exporting much faster.
  • Chapters become jump marks in the video.
Showing it

Present with a projector

The show runs fullscreen on the projector, while the laptop shows the current and the next slide, the time and the controls.

  • Kiosk mode starts the app straight into a slideshow in a loop – e.g. for an open day.
  • Pack project: everything in one folder for a USB stick and another PC.

And also

  • Photos and videos from anywhere

    iPhone (HEIC, Live Photos, HDR videos), Android (Motion Photos), cameras such as Sony (RAW, XAVC) and many more formats.

  • Video editing

    Trimming, slow motion and time lapse, and steadying shaky phone videos.

  • Safe projects

    Automatic backups, and damaged projects can be repaired.

  • Usable by everyone

    Light or dark, fully usable with the keyboard, with names for screen readers.

  • Built-in handbook

    F1 opens the handbook in the app, in German and English, offline too.

  • Play a folder

    Show a folder as a slideshow right away – no project needed.

Everything stays on your PC

Photos of pupils don't belong in a cloud. Diashow Studio works entirely on your computer.

  • No account, no cloud

    Photos, videos and projects stay on your PC. Projects are kept in Documents\Diashow Studio.

  • No telemetry

    No ads, no tracking, no usage statistics. The app's windows can't reach the internet at all.

  • Face detection offline

    Faces are detected and pixelated on your PC; no picture leaves it.

  • Only one internet request

    If you like, the app asks GitHub at most once a day whether there is a new version and then shows a hint. Nothing is installed automatically, and the check can be switched off in Settings.

  • Sharing on the local Wi-Fi only

    Share over Wi-Fi works without internet and only while its dialog is open.

  • Diagnostics only on request

    The error log stays on the PC. A diagnostics package is only made when you ask for it – and you see every file in it first.

System requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11

    64-bit. Installing needs no administrator rights.

  • Graphics card: optional

    With NVIDIA, Intel or AMD (NVENC, Quick Sync, AMF) exporting is much faster. Without one, everything runs on the processor; a smaller resolution such as 720p helps then.

  • System check for school PCs

    Settings → Troubleshooting → System check tests the graphics card, video encoders and memory and runs a short export test – each with a plain verdict.

“Windows protected your PC”?

On the first start, Windows SmartScreen may warn you because the app is new and has no paid code signature. To start it anyway:

  1. Click More info.
  2. Click Run anyway.

The warning appears for programs Windows hasn't seen often yet. The source code of Diashow Studio is public on GitHub.
